## What this project is about

This is a sales performance analysis of the Sample Superstore dataset, covering four years of order data (2014–2017). The aim was to look at how revenue and profit have changed over time, which products and regions are driving performance, and where the business has room to improve.

---

## Dataset

Sample - Superstore.xlsx — a popular retail dataset with ~10,000 orders across product categories, customer segments, and US regions. Source: Kaggle / Tableau Sample Data.

## What I analysed

1. **Data quality check** — duplicates, missing values, negative sales and profit
2. **Monthly revenue trend** across all four years
3. **Year-on-year** revenue and profit comparison
4. **Category and sub-category** performance (revenue vs profit)
5. **Regional breakdown** — which regions lead and which need attention
6. **Top 10 products** by revenue
7. **Sales vs profit scatter** by category — to show where losses are concentrated

---

## What stood out

- Revenue grew 51% between 2014 and 2017, with consistent Q4 peaks every year
- Technology is the strongest category — 17% profit margin and $145K total profit
- Furniture is a concern — $742K in revenue but only $18K profit (2.5% margin), driven by discounts as high as 80%
- 1,871 transactions recorded a loss — mostly in Furniture
- The West region leads in both revenue ($725K) and profit ($108K); the South has the lowest revenue at $392K
- The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Copier is the single highest-revenue product

---

## Recommendations

- Review the discount strategy for Furniture — the margins are not sustainable
- Invest more in Technology, particularly high-value products
- The South region needs a targeted marketing push
- Plan inventory and campaigns ahead of Q4 — it's consistently the strongest quarter
- Audit the Central region — high revenue but low profit points to over-discounting
